How to verify an Omarchy Linux Download

Visit the official Omarchy download page and click download Omarchy

Click on the sha-256 link.

Go to the Linux ISO Checker homepage.

Click "browse files" or drag the Omarchy ISO image from your Downloads folder into the "Select Local Linux ISO Image" box.

Click on the "Browse" button next to "Upload Manifest File" and choose the downloaded checksum file.

Click "Verify" to compare the calculated checksum with the expected value.

Depending on the file size, the verification process may take a minute or two to process.

When the process has completed, you will see the results of the verification.
