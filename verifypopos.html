How to verify a Pop!_OS Linux Download

Visit the official Pop!_OS download page to obtain your ISO file.

Click on the download link of the version of Pop!_OS you want to download. (i.e. Pop!_OS 24.04 LTS, Pop!_OS 24.04 LTS with NVIDIA, Pop!_OS 24.04 LTS for ARM)

Go to the Linux ISO Checker homepage.

Click "browse files" or drag the Pop!_OS ISO image from your Downloads folder into the "Select Local Linux ISO Image" box.

Copy the checksum from the Pop!_OS download page for the version you downloaded and paste it into the "Expected Checksum" field.

Click "Verify" to compare the calculated checksum with the expected value.

Depending on the file size, the verification process may take a minute or two to process.

When the process has completed, you will see the results of the verification.
