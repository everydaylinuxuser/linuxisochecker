How to verify a Manjaro Linux Download

Visit the official Manjaro Linux download page to obtain your ISO file.

Click on the download link of the version of Manjaro Linux you want to download. (i.e. KDE, XFCE, GNOME)

Click on the more link of the version of Manjaro you downloaded and click on the checksum

Go to the Linux ISO Checker homepage.

Click "browse files" or drag the Manjaro Linux ISO image from your Downloads folder into the "Select Local Linux ISO Image" box.

Copy the checksum into the checksum box.

Click "Verify" to compare the calculated checksum with the expected value.

Depending on the file size, the verification process may take a minute or two to process.

When the process has completed, you will see the results of the verification.
